## 3D Models

### Robot Kyle | URP
- **Publisher:** Unity Technologies
- **Source:** https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/characters/robots/robot-kyle-urp-4696
- **Version:** 4.0 (Aug 7, 2024)
- **Price:** Free
- **License:** Unity Asset Store EULA v. 1.0 ("Extension Asset" type)
  - https://unity.com/legal/as-terms
- **Usage in project:** Player character model (replaces the capsule visual)
- **Key EULA restrictions:**
  - May be used in games/media created with Unity, commercial included
  - May NOT resell or redistribute the raw assets (models, textures) on their own
  - One license per user seat; do not share the package between accounts/teams
